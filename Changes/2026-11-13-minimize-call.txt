================================================================================
Zeno TeamSuite — Change Summary
Date: 2026-11-13
Topic: Video calls — minimize the call and keep using the app (like Teams)
================================================================================

Request: while in a call, be able to minimize it and move around Zeno
TeamSuite, so the screen share can show the app (Backlog, Board, Test cases…)
instead of a full-screen call window.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) CALL WINDOW — Minimize / Expand
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Page name : Call window (opened from Chats → Call / Join call)
Route     : any page in the app once the call is minimized
Files:
  - frontend/src/components/chat/call-context.tsx   (NEW)
  - frontend/src/components/chat/call-room.tsx
  - frontend/src/components/chat/call-room.css
  - frontend/src/app/(app)/layout.tsx
  - frontend/src/app/(app)/chat/page.tsx

What changed:
  - After joining, the call header has a "Minimize" button (top right).
  - Minimized, the call becomes a small floating window in the bottom-right
    corner. It shows:
      • one video tile: someone's shared screen first, otherwise another
        person, otherwise you
      • "N in call", and a blue "Sharing" tag while you share your screen
      • buttons: microphone, camera, share screen, leave, and expand (↗)
  - The call STAYS CONNECTED while you open any other page (Backlog, Board,
    Issues, AI generator…). Only Leave, signing out, or a full page reload
    ends it.
  - The expand button (↗) brings back the full call screen.
  - In Chats, while you are in that project's call, the buttons say
    "Return to call".
  - You can be in one call at a time. Starting a call in another project shows
    "You are already in the <project> call. Leave it before joining another."
  - Fix: the name box on the "check your camera and microphone" screen is now
    hidden. Your name always comes from your account.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST IDEAS FOR BA / QA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Positive:
  - Join a call and press Minimize. A small window appears bottom-right, and
    the app is fully usable.
  - While minimized, open Backlog, then Board, then an issue. The window stays,
    and the other person can still hear you.
  - Minimize, press Share screen, pick the ENTIRE screen, and move around the
    app. The other person sees the TeamSuite pages you open. Your window shows
    "Sharing".
  - Press ↗ (expand). The full call screen returns, still connected.
  - Press Leave in the small window. It closes; Chats no longer shows "Call in
    progress" (within 10 s).
  - In Chats, while in the call: the header button and the green bar say
    "Return to call".
Negative:
  - While in project A's call, open project B's chat and press Call. You see
    "You are already in the A call…" and A's call comes back.
  - Reload the page (F5) during a call. The call ends (expected); join again
    from Chats.
Regression:
  - The pre-join screen shows the camera preview, Microphone, Camera and Join
    call, with no name box.
  - The full call screen still has share screen, chat, settings and leave.
  - Cancelling screen share shows no error (see 2026-11-13-screen-share-cancel.txt).

Checked on 2026-11-13 in Chrome against a local LiveKit server. Join →
Minimize → Backlog → Board → Expand → Leave works, and the server showed the
person still connected throughout, then 0 people after Leave.
