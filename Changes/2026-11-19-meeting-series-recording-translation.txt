================================================================================
Zeno TeamSuite — Change Summary
Date: 2026-11-19
Topic: Repeating meetings, meeting chat, Soniox call recording (transcripts)
       and live translation in calls
App: Zeno TeamSuite (frontend Next.js + backend FastAPI + LiveKit + Soniox)
================================================================================

1. Meetings can repeat (every day, weekday, week, 2 weeks or month) until a
   chosen date, for up to one year.
2. Every meeting has its own chat.
3. In a video call (meeting or project), Record transcribes the call with
   Soniox AI. When it stops, the transcript appears in the meeting's chat (or
   the project chat for project calls).
4. In a call, Translate shows live captions of what the others say,
   translated into the language you pick.

Note: "Record" saves a written transcript (who said what, as text). It does
not save an audio or video file. Video recording would need a separate
LiveKit Egress service on the server.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) REPEATING MEETINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Page name : Calendar → Schedule meeting / Edit / meeting details
Route     : /calendar
Files:
  - frontend/src/components/calendar/meeting-ui.tsx
  - frontend/src/lib/meetings.ts
  - backend/app/services/meetings.py, backend/app/schemas/meeting.py
  - backend/app/migrations/versions/o9h3_meeting_series_chat.py   (DB migration)

What changed:
  - New meeting form: "Repeat" with Does not repeat / Every day / Every
    weekday (Mon–Fri) / Every week / Every 2 weeks / Every month.
  - When repeating, an "Until" date appears with quick buttons: 1 week,
    1 month, 3 months, 6 months. The default is 1 month.
  - Rules: the end date must be after the first meeting, and at most one year
    away. At most 100 meetings are created per series.
  - Monthly on the 29th–31st: in shorter months the meeting is on the last day.
  - Times stay at the same local clock time, even across daylight saving.
  - Each date is a normal meeting on the calendar, with its own call and chat.
    A repeat icon shows next to the title, and the details say "Repeats every
    week until Dec 29, 2026".
  - Edit a repeating meeting: "Apply changes to" offers This meeting only /
    This and following meetings / All meetings in the series. A new time
    moves the other dates by the same amount.
  - Cancel a repeating meeting: choose This one / This and following / All,
    or "Keep" to back out.
  - Invited people get ONE notification for the whole series ("Repeats every
    week until …"), not one per date.
  - The repeat pattern itself cannot be changed after creating. To change it,
    cancel "All" and schedule again.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2) MEETING CHAT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Page name : Calendar → open a meeting → "Chat and transcripts" tab
Route     : /calendar?meeting=<id>&tab=chat
Files:
  - frontend/src/components/calendar/meeting-chat.tsx              (NEW)
  - backend/app/services/transcripts.py                            (NEW)

What changed:
  - The meeting dialog now has two tabs: Details, and Chat and transcripts.
  - Only the organizer and invited people (and admins) can read and post.
  - New messages notify the other meeting people (bell / desktop). Clicking
    the notification opens the meeting on the chat tab.
  - Refreshes every 10 seconds.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3) RECORD A CALL (SONIOX TRANSCRIPT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Where     : inside any video call (meeting call or project call), top bar
Files:
  - frontend/src/components/chat/call-tools.tsx                    (NEW)
  - frontend/src/lib/call-audio.ts                                 (NEW)
  - frontend/src/components/chat/call-room.tsx, call-context.tsx
  - frontend/src/lib/soniox-live.ts
  - backend/app/api/transcripts.py                                 (NEW)

What changed:
  - "● Record" button in the call's top bar. Pressing it transcribes
    everyone's voices (yours and the others') with Soniox AI. Speakers are
    labelled "Speaker 1", "Speaker 2"… and mixed languages are detected.
  - While recording, everyone in the call sees a red "Recording" badge (with
    the recorder's name), and the minimized call shows a red dot.
  - The button changes to "■ Stop recording · 3:12" with a timer.
  - Only one person can record a call at a time. For the others, Record is
    greyed out with "<name> is recording".
  - The text is saved every 10 seconds. On Stop (or when the recorder leaves
    the call) the transcript is finished, and:
      • meeting call → it appears in the meeting's "Chat and transcripts"
      • project call → it appears in that project's chat
      • the others get a "Transcript of … is ready" notification
  - A transcript card shows who recorded it, when, how long and the languages.
    Click it to read the text, with Copy and Download .txt buttons.
  - A recording cut off (tab closed, network lost) keeps the text saved so far
    and is marked "(recording was cut off)".
  - Delete: the recorder, the meeting organizer / project lead, or an admin.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4) LIVE TRANSLATION IN CALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Where     : inside any video call, top bar → "Translate" with a language list

What changed:
  - Choose a language (English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam,
    Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Urdu, Spanish, French, German,
    Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, and more), or Off.
  - Captions appear at the bottom of the video: the translation in large
    text, and what was originally said in small grey text.
  - Each person picks their own language. It only affects their screen, and
    the browser remembers the choice for next time.
  - Only other people's voices are translated, not your own.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SETUP / DEPLOY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - DB migration o9h3_meeting_series_chat runs automatically on deploy.
  - New Python package: tzdata (time zones). It installs with the image build.
  - Recording and translation use the existing SONIOX_API_KEY. If it is not
    set, pressing Record / Translate shows an error.
  - Soniox model default changed from stt-rt-v5 (retired by Soniox on
    Dec 15, 2026) to stt-rt-v6.
  - Each recording or translation is a separate Soniox stream, billed per
    minute of audio by Soniox.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST HINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Repeating meetings
  1. Schedule "Standup", Every weekday, Until "1 week" → about 5–6 meetings
     appear, none on Saturday or Sunday.
  2. Every month starting Jan 31 until April 30 → Jan 31, Feb 28, Mar 31,
     Apr 30.
  3. Until date before the start, or more than a year away → red message, and
     Schedule is disabled.
  4. Edit the 3rd date with "This meeting only" → only that date changes.
     Edit with "This and following" and a 1 hour later start → the 3rd and
     later dates move 1 hour; earlier dates stay.
  5. Cancel "This and following" on the 4th date → dates 4 and later disappear.
  6. The invited person gets exactly one notification for the series.

Meeting chat
  7. An invited person posts → the organizer sees it, plus a notification
     that opens the chat tab.
  8. Someone not invited cannot open the meeting or its chat.

Recording (needs two people in a call, and Soniox configured)
  9. Person A presses Record → both A and B see the red Recording badge.
     B's Record button is greyed out.
 10. Speak in English and Hindi, then Stop → "Transcript saved to the
     meeting chat". The card shows both languages, and the text has
     Speaker 1 / Speaker 2.
 11. B gets a "Transcript of … is ready" notification.
 12. A closes the tab while recording → the transcript keeps the text up to
     about 10 seconds before, and after 2 minutes shows "recording was cut
     off". Someone else can now record.
 13. In a project call, Record → the transcript shows in the project chat.

Translation
 14. B picks Hindi under Translate, and A speaks English → B sees Hindi
     captions with the English underneath. A sees nothing (A's choice is Off).
 15. Choose Off → captions disappear.
