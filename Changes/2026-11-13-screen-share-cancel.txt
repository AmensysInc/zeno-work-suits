================================================================================
Zeno TeamSuite — Change Summary
Date: 2026-11-13
Topic: Video calls — screen sharing fixes (cancel error, "mirror" effect)
================================================================================

Problem reported: in a call, pressing "Share screen" and then Cancel in the
browser's picker showed "Could not use your camera or microphone. Check the
browser permission." Nothing was wrong: cancelling is a normal choice.

Problem 2 reported: when sharing, the screen showed many copies of itself inside
each other (a "hall of mirrors"). Your own shared screen was shown back to you
inside the call, and that call was itself on the screen being shared.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) CHATS — Call window
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Page name : Chats → Call (full-screen call window)
Route     : /chat?project=<projectId>
Files:
  - frontend/src/components/chat/call-room.tsx
  - frontend/src/components/chat/call-room.css      (NEW)

What changed:
  - Like Teams, you no longer see your own shared screen in the call. Your
    screen-share tile shows "You are sharing your screen. Others can see it."
    No more repeated copies. Everyone else still sees your screen normally.
  - Cancelling the screen-share picker shows no message.
  - If screen sharing really fails, the message is "Screen sharing could not
    start. Try again."
  - Camera and microphone problems now say which device and what to do:
      • blocked   → "Your camera is blocked. Allow it in the browser's site
                     settings (the icon next to the address bar)."
      • missing   → "No microphone was found on this device."
      • in use    → "Your camera is being used by another app. Close it and
                     try again."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST IDEAS FOR BA / QA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Two people in a call. Person A shares the ENTIRE screen.
      → A sees "You are sharing your screen. Others can see it." (no repeated copies).
      → B sees A's screen clearly, with no repeated copies.
  - A shares just one window or one browser tab. B sees it; A sees the message.
  - A stops sharing. The tile disappears and the camera grid returns.
  - In a call, press Share screen, then Cancel. No error appears and the call
    continues.
  - Press Share screen and pick a window. It shares. Stop sharing. No error.
  - Block the camera in the site settings (the icon next to the address bar),
    then turn the camera on. The "camera is blocked" message appears.
  - On a PC with no microphone, join. The "No microphone was found" message
    appears.
  - With the camera open in another app (e.g. Teams or Zoom on Windows), turn
    the camera on in our call. The "being used by another app" message appears.
