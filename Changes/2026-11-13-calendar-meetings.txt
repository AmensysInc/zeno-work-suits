================================================================================
Zeno TeamSuite — Change Summary
Date: 2026-11-13
Topic: Calendar — schedule meetings, invite people, join the meeting's video call
App: Zeno TeamSuite (frontend Next.js + backend FastAPI + LiveKit)
================================================================================

A new Calendar page sits directly below Home in the left menu. People can
schedule meetings, invite anyone in the company, and join each meeting's own
video call (which can be minimized like project calls).

Decisions (agreed 2026-11-13):
  - Attendees: anyone in the company. Tagging a project is optional.
  - Every meeting has its own private video call, with a Join button.
  - Invited people see the meeting on their Calendar and on Home. No email yet.
  - Repeating meetings: not in this version.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) CALENDAR PAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Page name : Calendar
Route     : /calendar        (left menu: Home → Calendar)
Files:
  - frontend/src/app/(app)/calendar/page.tsx                   (NEW)
  - frontend/src/components/calendar/meeting-ui.tsx            (NEW)
  - frontend/src/lib/meetings.ts                               (NEW)
  - frontend/src/components/layout/app-shell.tsx   (menu link)

What changed:
  - Month view (Monday to Sunday), with Today and ‹ › buttons to change month.
    Today's date is circled.
  - Each day shows up to 3 meetings ("10:30 AM Sprint review"), then "+N more".
    On phones, days show dots instead.
  - Click a day to list its meetings on the right. Double-click a day to
    schedule on that day.
  - Click a meeting to see its details: date and time, project, agenda, and
    people (the organizer is marked). Buttons: Join, Edit, Cancel meeting.
  - "Schedule meeting" (top right) or "New" opens the form:
      • Title (required)
      • Date, Start, End. Default is the next half hour, 30 minutes long. The
        length is shown under End. Maximum 12 hours. An end time earlier than
        the start means the meeting runs past midnight.
      • Invite people: search and tick anyone in the company. Picked people
        show as chips with an ×. The organizer is always included.
      • Project (optional), Agenda (optional)
  - Times are saved in UTC and shown in each person's own time zone.
  - The list refreshes every minute.

3) MEETING VIDEO CALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Files:
  - frontend/src/components/chat/call-context.tsx
  - frontend/src/components/chat/call-room.tsx
  - frontend/src/app/(app)/chat/page.tsx
  - backend/app/services/calls.py

What changed:
  - Each meeting has its own private call room, separate from the project chat
    calls. Only the organizer and invited people can join (admins can too).
  - Join opens the same call window as Chats: device check, then the call.
    Minimize keeps it running while you use other pages. While you are in it,
    the button says "Return to call".
  - Join is highlighted from 15 minutes before the start until the end. The
    call can be opened at any time from the meeting details.
  - One call at a time, the same rule as project calls.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4) WHO CAN DO WHAT — Server rules
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
API:
  GET    /api/meetings?start=&end=   your meetings in a range (max 62 days)
  POST   /api/meetings               schedule
  GET    /api/meetings/{id}          details
  PUT    /api/meetings/{id}          edit (organizer or admin)
  DELETE /api/meetings/{id}          cancel (organizer or admin)
  GET    /api/meetings/people        everyone who can be invited (names only, no emails)
  GET    /api/meetings/{id}/call, POST /api/meetings/{id}/call/join
Files:
  - backend/app/models/meeting.py, backend/app/schemas/meeting.py   (NEW)
  - backend/app/services/meetings.py, backend/app/api/meetings.py   (NEW)
  - backend/app/migrations/versions/m7f1_meetings.py                (NEW, database change)
  - backend/tests/test_meetings.py                                  (NEW, 10 tests)

Rules:
  - You see meetings you organize or are invited to. Admins can open any
    meeting by link, but their calendar shows only their own meetings.
  - Only the organizer (or an admin) can edit or cancel a meeting.
  - You can tag only a project you can open.
  - Invalid input is rejected: end before start, longer than 12 hours, a blank
    title, or unknown people.

TEST IDEAS FOR BA / QA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Use two people, A and B.
Positive:
  - The left menu shows Calendar under Home. Open it: the current month shows
    with today circled.
  - A: Schedule meeting → title "Sprint review", today, the next half hour,
    invite B → Schedule. The meeting shows on today's date and in the right
    panel.
  - B: open Calendar. The same meeting is there. On Home, "Upcoming meetings"
    lists it.
  - Within 15 minutes of the start, the row is green with Join. A and B both
    join and can see and hear each other. Minimize, then open other pages: the
    call continues.
  - A: open the meeting → Edit → change the time and invite C → Save. B and C
    see the new time.
  - A: Cancel meeting → "Yes, cancel meeting". It disappears for everyone.
  - Use ‹ › to go to the next month and back. Today returns to the current
    month.
  - Double-click a future day. The form opens on that date at 10:00 AM.
Negative:
  - Person C (not invited) cannot see the meeting or join its call.
  - B (invited, not the organizer) sees Join but no Edit or Cancel buttons.
  - End time 12+ hours after the start: "A meeting can be at most 12 hours
    long".
  - An empty title: the Schedule button stays disabled.
Time zones:
  - Someone in another time zone sees the same meeting at their own local time.
Regression:
  - Calls from Chats work as before ("Return to call", minimize).
  - Home page tiles and charts are unchanged below the new card.

Checked on 2026-11-13 in Chrome against a local LiveKit server:
  - Calendar → Schedule, inviting Mia → shown on the 13th, in the day panel and
    on Home.
  - Mia's calendar lists it.
  - Join → Minimize → Home: the meeting room showed 1 person, and the project
    chat room stayed empty.
  - The details show the people and Edit/Cancel. Leave empties the room.
  - No console errors.
